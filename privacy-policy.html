Privacy Summary at a Glance

RNR Ads respects corporate and individual privacy. We strictly use client inquiries to provide transit advertising quotes, execute outdoor campaigns across Hyderabad, and deliver geotagged audit proofs. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or brand data to third-party telemarketers.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all services and digital touchpoints operated by RNR Ads ("we", "us", or "our"), headquartered at 413, 4th Floor, Kubera Towers, Narayanaguda, Hyderabad - 500029, Telangana, India, including our website rnrads.com, campaign proposal communications, client portal interactions, and field audit documentation.

By requesting a quotation, utilizing our transit advertising services (including auto-rickshaw branding and TSRTC city bus wraps), or interacting with our online platforms, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide tailored outdoor media plans and verify transit advertising deployments, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Direct Business Inquiries: Your full name, corporate organization / brand name, official email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and specific advertising preferences submitted via quote forms.
  • Campaign Assets & Creatives: Vector logos, creative artwork files, high-resolution branding graphics, and product taglines supplied for print production.
  • Billing & GST Details: Registered company legal name, GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number), billing address, and transaction records for invoices.
  • Automated Website Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs collected through standard server logs and privacy-preserving analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate operational and business purposes:

Quotation & Planning
Generating accurate fleet estimates, route schedules, and pricing packages tailored to your commercial requirements.
Fleet Production
Coordinating large-format vinyl printing, lamination, and mounting on auto-rickshaws and TSRTC buses.
Geotagged Auditing
Compiling and delivering comprehensive time-stamped proof-of-performance photographic audit decks.
Client Support
Direct phone, email, and WhatsApp communications regarding live campaign monitoring and renewals.

4. Transit Media Photography & Geotagged Audits

As part of our outdoor advertising verification protocol across Hyderabad:

  • Our ground verification teams capture photographic proof of installed auto-rickshaw hoods, rear panels, and TSRTC bus wraps in public thoroughfares.
  • These photos document the vehicle registration number, route placement, and advertisement quality for audit transparency.
  • Campaign photographs may be featured in our portfolio or case studies with client permission. Any sensitive proprietary data will be redacted upon request.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell your personal or corporate data to any third party. We only share information under strict confidentiality circumstances:

  • Transport Authorities: Sharing required vehicle registration and advertisement layout permits with municipal bodies (GHMC) or transit corporations (TSRTC) for regulatory outdoor advertising compliance.
  • Print & Fabrication Partners: Transmitting approved client artwork to high-resolution vinyl printing and lamination facilities under strict non-disclosure obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: Disclosing details if mandated by applicable law, court order, or governmental authorities in Telangana or India.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes standard session cookies to enhance navigation, remember form inputs, and evaluate aggregate web traffic. You have full freedom to disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the core accessibility of our transit media listings.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect all client contact information, graphic files, and billing data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

Campaign documentation and audit decks are retained for the duration of the active contract and for a statutory period required by Indian tax and commercial laws.

8. Your Legal Rights

As a valued client or website visitor, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal or commercial contact information we hold about your business.
  • Request corrections or updates to inaccurate billing or contact records.
  • Opt out of promotional campaign announcements or email newsletters at any time.
  • Request deletion of non-statutory records upon campaign completion.
