Why auto rickshaw transit advertising is Hyderabad's highest ROI outdoor media channel.

Dynamic Mobile Visibility

Auto rickshaw ads are one of the most dynamic forms of outdoor advertising. At RNR Ads, we specialize in turning these everyday vehicles into powerful branding tools. As autos move through busy streets and residential areas, they offer continuous exposure to your message, reaching a broad and diverse audience that static billboards cannot match.

Unmatched Versatility & Placement

In addition to their mobility, auto ads are highly versatile. You can opt for full-vehicle wraps for maximum impact or choose back and side panel ads for more focused messaging. Either way, your brand gains visibility in high-traffic areas, markets, and neighborhoods that other static ads frequently miss.

High Dwell-Time & Urban Resonance

Furthermore, our carefully designed auto ads ensure that your brand stands out, capturing attention at every stop and turn. Not only do they help businesses gain more recognition, but they also create a lasting impression that resonates with commuters and pedestrians alike across all Greater Hyderabad zones.

Cost-Effective & Scalable Reach

Moreover, our ads offer complete flexibility. You can choose between full-body wraps, back panel ads, or side banners—each crafted to reflect your brand's personality. Whether you're a local shop or a major national brand, our clients trust us to transform auto rickshaws into cost-effective, mobile advertising tools that bring visibility to every corner of the city.
