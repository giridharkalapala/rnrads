Commercial Agreement Summary

These Terms of Service govern all transit advertising media buy orders placed with RNR Ads. All campaigns include certified high-grade vinyl printing, professional vehicle fleet mounting across Greater Hyderabad, free routine maintenance replacement for damaged media, and geotagged photographic audit verification decks.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By signing a Campaign Release Order (RO), submitting a digital quotation request, executing a purchase agreement, or making a campaign advance payment to RNR Ads ("Agency", "we", "us"), the client ("Advertiser", "Brand", "Client") agrees to be legally bound by these Terms of Service.

Our registered operational office is located at 413, 4th Floor, Kubera Towers, Narayanaguda, Hyderabad - 500029, Telangana, India.

2. Campaign Booking & Estimates

  • Proposals & Quotations: All initial rate proposals, zone allocations, and inventory estimates remain valid for 15 calendar days from the date of quotation issuance.
  • Inventory Reservation: Vehicle inventory (Auto Rickshaws and TSRTC City Buses) is confirmed and reserved only upon receipt of formal written approval (Release Order / Email Confirmation) accompanied by the agreed advance payment.
  • Minimum Order Fleet: Auto-rickshaw campaigns require a minimum of 50 vehicles for targeted geographic coverage. TSRTC Bus Branding requires a minimum of 5 buses per route corridor.

3. Artwork & Creative Approval

The Advertiser is responsible for supplying final, print-ready digital artwork in high-resolution vector format (.AI, .EPS, .CDR, or high-DPI .PDF) formatted to our vehicle dimension specifications.

  • Digital Proof Sign-Off: RNR Ads will produce a scaled 2D vehicle digital proof. Production commences only after explicit written approval from the Client.
  • Advertiser Warranties: The Advertiser warrants that all supplied artwork, logos, copy, and trademarks are owned or legitimately licensed by the Advertiser and do not infringe on any third-party intellectual property.

4. Print Production, Vinyl Standards & Fleet Mounting

All transit branding executed by RNR Ads uses certified outdoor media specifications:

Cast & Monomeric Vinyl
High-tensile vinyl with weather-sealed UV laminate designed to withstand Hyderabad's sun, dust, and rain.
Eco-Solvent High DPI Print
Vibrant, photo-realistic color fidelity ensuring crisp typography and brand color accuracy.
Professional Fabrication
Executed by certified mounting specialists at dedicated transit depots across Greater Hyderabad.

5. Proof of Performance & Photographic Audits

RNR Ads guarantees 100% transparent audit reporting for every vehicle deployed in the campaign:

  • Within 72 hours of campaign execution, the Advertiser receives a comprehensive digital audit deck.
  • Each entry includes high-resolution, time-stamped, geotagged photographs showing the vehicle registration number, route placement, and ad visibility.
  • Mid-campaign spot inspection reports are provided for long-duration contracts (60+ days).

6. Fleet Maintenance & Free Damage Replacement

Due to daily on-road driving conditions in urban Hyderabad, transit media may occasionally suffer road scuffs or accidental wear.

  • Active Maintenance Guarantee: RNR Ads maintains an extra buffer of reprinted vinyl graphics. Any reported damaged ad is inspected, reprinted, and re-mounted within 48 hours at zero extra charge to the Advertiser.
  • Fleet Availability: In the rare event a vehicle is retired from active service by its owner during a campaign, RNR Ads promptly replaces it with another licensed vehicle on the identical route.

7. Billing, GST & Payment Schedules

Standard payment terms for outdoor transit media campaigns:

  • Advance Payment: 50% mobilization advance upon Release Order signing and artwork approval before print production begins.
  • Balance Payment: Remaining 50% upon delivery of the geotagged photographic audit deck within 7 working days of campaign deployment.
  • Applicable Taxes: All rates are subject to 18% GST (Goods & Services Tax) in compliance with Indian commercial tax regulations. Tax invoices with GSTIN will be provided.

8. Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

  • Pre-Production Cancellation: Cancellations made before vinyl printing incurs only a 10% administrative processing charge.
  • Post-Production Cancellation: If print production has already commenced, printing and material costs are non-refundable. Remaining mounting/fleet fees will be refunded pro-rata.
  • Live Campaign Cancellation: Once vehicles are active on the road, live campaign fees cannot be cancelled mid-month.

9. Advertising Standards & Regulatory Compliance

All transit advertisements must comply strictly with the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) code, Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) outdoor advertising bylaws, and TSRTC transit guidelines.

We reserve the right to decline or request modifications to artwork promoting prohibited goods, misleading claims, political defamation, or content violating local public morality guidelines.

10. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms of Service and any commercial contracts arising under them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Telangana and the Republic of India.

Any legal disputes or proceedings shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent civil courts in Hyderabad, Telangana.
