FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the minimum fleet size required to launch a campaign?

For Auto Rickshaw Branding, our minimum package starts from 50 vehicles for targeted local coverage, and scales up to 5,000+ autos for city-wide dominance. For TSRTC City Bus Branding, packages start from 5 buses.

How do you verify and prove that the vehicles are on the road?

Every vehicle deployment includes a comprehensive audit report with high-resolution, geotagged, time-stamped photographs showing your advertisement, vehicle registration number, and driver details.

What happens if an ad sticker gets damaged during the campaign?

We provide free replacement maintenance. Our ground audit team conducts regular inspections, and any damaged or peeling vinyl is reprinted and re-applied within 48 hours at zero extra charge.

How quickly can our campaign go live on Hyderabad streets?

Once your artwork design is finalized and approved, our in-house printing and fleet fabrication team can produce and deploy your campaign across Hyderabad in as fast as 48 to 72 hours.
