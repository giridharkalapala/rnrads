OUR STORY & FOUNDATION

Hyderabad’s Lifeline.
Your Brand’s Greatest Moving Canvas.

Founded with a commitment to make outdoor advertising transparent, hyper-targeted, and results-driven, RNR Ads has grown into the Twin Cities' most trusted auto rickshaw transit media house.

In a fast-growing metropolis like Hyderabad, static hoardings are increasingly overlooked by commuters focused on the road. Auto rickshaws, however, weave through every bustling commercial hub, tech park gate, arterial flyover, and residential street — placing your brand message directly at eye-level where captive audiences cannot look away.

By working directly with auto driver unions and maintaining dedicated operational hubs in 30+ strategic localities, we eliminate middlemen markups and guarantee rapid campaign rollout with timestamped photographic audits.
