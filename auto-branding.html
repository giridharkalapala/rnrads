Auto Rickshaw Advertising Formats

Tailored transit media formats designed for high impact, maximum dwell time, and unmissable brand presence.

FORMAT 01

Full Auto Branding (360° Wrap)

The pinnacle of outdoor vehicle advertising. Full auto branding wraps the entire auto rickshaw including the top canopy hood, back flap, side body panels, and driver partition board.

  • 360-degree brand visibility from all driving angles
  • High-resolution UV-cured vinyl printing
  • Premium lamination protecting against rain, heat, and dust
Get Quote For Full Wrap
FORMAT 02

Side Panel Advertising

Side panel ads position your message at direct eye-level for pedestrians, commuters on sidewalks, shoppers in busy market corridors, and vehicles traveling alongside.

  • Dual side placement (Left & Right passenger sides)
  • Perfect for app install prompts, QR codes, and retail offers
  • High frequency in shopping, university, and commercial zones
Get Quote For Side Panels
FORMAT 03

Rear Panel Advertising (Back Hood)

The most popular and cost-effective transit format in India. Rear panel ads provide captive dwell time for motorists, cars, bikes, and bus commuters stopped behind auto rickshaws at Hyderabad's traffic signals.

  • Average 90+ seconds uninterrupted dwell time at signals
  • Mounted on rigid back boards with heavy-duty metal fixtures
  • Lowest Cost-Per-Thousand (CPM) impressions
Get Quote For Rear Panels
